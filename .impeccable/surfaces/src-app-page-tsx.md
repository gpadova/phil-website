# Surface: / (landing page) — src/app/page.tsx

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Single landing page for planwithpinpoint.com. Visitor: a person with $300K–$1M of discretionary money who saw a short video and is checking whether Phil is credible and whether this is what they think it is. Action: "I'd like to learn more" → request an introductory conversation (form → email; booking link to be swapped in when Phil confirms one). Proof on hand: Phil himself (portrait, his own analogies and voice), his process, the general-market arithmetic (IRA/401(k) caps, 59½, 10% penalty, the 2008 drop-vs-recovery math, brokerage ~1%/yr). Constraint: no carrier illustration figures on the page; concepts on the page, numbers in the room.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the bank Phil tells you to become. A banking hall, not a brochure: the visitor walks into a green-marble hall where the numbers are kept behind a vault door and the one teller window open is Phil's. It refuses the category arrangement (navy hero + "secure your future" + three icon cards + trust badges) and the fintech opposite (black + neon glow).

OWN-WORLD: Banker's green marble owns the walls (deep #0d3629 hall, #17503f veined lighter panels); travertine (#efe8da) is the counter and every document laid on it; brushed brass (#c9a24d, lit #e6c877) is every rail, rule, control edge and the one light source; ink #0b1210. Type: an inscriptional Roman capital (engraved into the marble, letterspaced, with a lit top edge) for the wordmark and hooks; a Futura-class geometric grotesque in caps for signage, labels, controls and tabular ledger numerals; a readable text serif for teaching paragraphs on travertine. Controls are teller windows: a brass frame with a travertine field; active state lights the brass from within. Rules are brass hairlines with a chamfered end. Ledger lines print in tabular figures. Portraits sit in brass-edged plates. With all copy removed you would still see: green stone, brass rails, a travertine counter, one vault.

STORY: "I don't need life insurance" → "this isn't life insurance the way I understood it" → "it grows, I can use it, it doesn't go down" → "this man clearly knows the mechanics and doesn't charge me to find out" → "I'd like to learn more." One action, repeated at the teller window (top rail), after the ledger, and at the vault.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green hall. Top rail: PINPOINT FINANCIAL engraved left, the lit teller window "I'd like to learn more" right. Left 7/12: the hook in engraved capitals at ~5rem desktop — "You don't need life insurance." then, one line lower and brass-lit, "You need what this does." Under it, one serif paragraph in Phil's register, ≤3 lines. Right 4/12: Phil's portrait in a tall brass-edged plate, caption "Phil Isan · Certified IUL Specialist · Delray Beach, FL". Bottom: a brass ledger rail spanning the width with three engraved entries — GROWTH (tax-advantaged, no cap) · ACCESS (borrow against it, keep it growing) · PROTECTION (a floor under every down year). Mobile: hook, portrait plate, rail stack in that order; the teller window stays in the top rail.

FORM: The Banking Hall — candidate 4 of 7 on the grounded list (ranked: passbook, engraved security, illustration table, banking hall, tax-code page, property survey, index chart). Seed key aef6e369, assigned index 4, chosen by the user on the decision page. Raises kept from declined challengers: numbered teaching panels with one highlighted line (zine); one continuous ledger that prints as you scroll and never posts a negative entry (terminal); light-from-inside as active state, one warm source (akari); the wordmark as one engraved mass (blackbook). Signature interaction: the vault door — a drawn circular door whose handle turns and which swings open as it scrolls into view, revealing the (blurred, unreadable) illustration ledger behind it, with the request form as the only way in. Motion grammar: brass lights up (inset glow + color) on hover/active; ledger rows print with a 40ms stagger and an exponential ease-out; the vault swings once; reduced-motion renders everything settled.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
